# Pizza Yard (بيتزا يارد)

## Client facts
- Type: pizza restaurant, physical location in 6th of October (Egypt).
- Started with us: Nov 2026. User handles media buying.
- Problem at start: ads not bringing results.
- Tried: Engagement campaign → no results (likes/comments, no orders).
- Relationship: client owners are the user's friends.
- Ad budget: very small, ~600–900 EGP (separate from marketing budget).
- Unknown yet (ask/fill in): delivery vs dine-in vs takeaway, on Talabat/Elmenus?,
  WhatsApp/hotline for orders, avg order value, delivery radius, current orders/day, pixel/website.

## Strategy v1 — Nov 2026 (SUPERSEDED by v2: budget too small for 3 campaigns)
Goal = orders, not engagement. Measure cost per order, not cost per like.

Campaign mix (Meta):
1. ~60-70% budget: **Engagement objective → Messages (WhatsApp)** / or Calls. Ad = offer + "اطلب على الواتساب".
   Main KPI: cost per conversation, then % of chats that become orders.
2. ~20-30%: **Awareness → Reach**, radius 3-5 km around branch (+ nearby compounds / Sheikh Zayed if delivered),
   short food video (cheese pull). Frequency cap ~2-3/week. Builds video-viewer audience.
3. ~10%: **Retargeting** — video viewers 50%+ / page+IG engagers / people who messaged → strong offer.
- If on Talabat/Elmenus: Traffic campaign to the listing link with a code to track.
- Dayparting: lunch 1-4pm, dinner 7pm-1am, push budget Thu/Fri.
- Students angle: universities in October (MSA, MUST, October Univ, Misr Univ, Nile Univ in Zayed) → student combo.

Creative rules: hook in first 2 sec (cheese pull / oven / slice pull), show price + offer + area clearly,
UGC/real customers, Reels 9:16. Rotate creatives every 2-3 weeks or when frequency > 3.

Tracking: unique promo code per campaign, WhatsApp label per campaign, cashier asks "شفتنا فين؟",
log daily orders before vs after.

Google: optimize Google Business Profile (photos, menu, reviews) + Google Ads local search "بيتزا أكتوبر / delivery".

## Strategy v2 — Nov 2026 (current, for 600–900 EGP budget)
Diagnosis: WhatsApp messages campaign brought chats but people ask and don't buy →
the leak is the chat/offer, not targeting. Fix the chat before spending more.

- ONE campaign, ONE ad set (Messages → WhatsApp), broad, 3–5 km radius, Advantage+ placements.
  Lifetime budget so ad scheduling works: run lunch 1–4pm + dinner 7pm–1am, heavier Thu/Fri.
  No audience splitting — budget can't feed multiple ad sets' learning.
- Personas = creative angles only (2–3 ads inside the same ad set), not separate ad sets:
  1) طلبة جامعات أكتوبر — cheap combo/slice deal.  2) عائلات الكمبوندات — family box weekend.
  3) سهرة/ماتش — match-night box (time ads around big football matches).
- Price + offer + delivery area visible IN the ad → filters out "بكام؟" chats.
- WhatsApp Business setup: greeting message + quick reply with menu photo, prices, offer, delivery fee/time,
  one clear question ("تحب نبعتها على عنوان فين؟"). Reply < 5 min. Follow up after 30–60 min with deadline offer.
- Track every chat: asked / ordered / why not (price, area, delivery time, no reply).
- Free levers alongside: Google Business Profile, organic Reels, boost the best organic Reel only.
- Success metric: cost per ORDER. Target chat→order rate ≥ 20–30% before scaling.
